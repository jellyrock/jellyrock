# Voice control

Use your Roku voice remote to search your libraries and to control what's playing without reaching for the buttons.

## What you need

A Roku voice remote. Roku decides which words it understands; JellyRock acts on the commands Roku passes on.

## Search by voice

- **On the Search screen**, speak instead of typing. JellyRock waits until you finish speaking, then searches for the whole phrase.
- **In a library**, speak while the library is open to show only the items whose names match. In the Live TV guide, the same narrows the channel list.

## Control playback by voice

While a video plays, you can ask your Roku to:

- **play**, **pause** or **stop**;
- **fast-forward** or **rewind**, or **skip forward or back** a number of seconds or minutes;
- **start over** from the beginning;
- **replay** the last few seconds; how far is set by [Instant Replay Duration](../app-settings.md#playbackInstantReplaySeconds);
- **skip**: skips the intro, recap or other marked part playing now (see [Media segments](media-segments.md)), or goes to the next item when nothing is marked;
- go to the **next** item;
- say **what's playing**;
- **like** it, which marks it as a favorite, or **dislike** it, which takes it off your favorites.

While music plays, the same commands work, and you can also **shuffle** or **repeat**.

If a command doesn't apply, for example pausing something already paused, Roku tells you so.

## Settings

- [Instant Replay Duration](../app-settings.md#playbackInstantReplaySeconds): how many seconds **replay** goes back. The default is 10.

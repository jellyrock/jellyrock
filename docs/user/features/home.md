# Home

Home is the first screen after you sign in. Its rows show what you were watching, the next episode of each show you follow, and what's new in each of your libraries.

## What's on Home

- **Continue Watching**: what you started and didn't finish.
- **Next Up**: the next episode of each show you're watching.
- **My Media**: your libraries. Select one to browse it.
- **Recently Added in** each library: its newest items.
- **On Now** and **Active Recordings**, when your server has Live TV and your account may watch it.

Home shows these rows in the order you set on your server's web client, on its **Home** settings page, unless you turn off [Use Web Client's Home Section Arrangement](../app-settings.md#uiHomeUseWebArrangement). A change to that setting shows the next time you sign in. The **Favorites** tab at the top of Home has your favorites. See [Favorites](favorites.md).

## Change Home from its menu

Select the settings icon at the top right of Home, or press **Star** (`*`) on your remote. A menu opens with Home's own settings above **All settings**, each showing its value:

- **Row Layout**: rows that run to the screen's edges, or the original inset rows.
- **Episode Images Next Up**: in Continue Watching and Next Up, the episode's own image, the show's, or whichever your web client is set to show.
- **Home Row Size**: how many items Recently Added, On Now and Active Recordings show.
- **Max Days Next Up**: how long a show stays in Next Up after you last watched it.
- **Rewatching Next Up**: whether Next Up shows episodes you've already watched.

Pick a new value and Home shows it at once. On the **Favorites** tab, the menu offers **Row Layout** alone. These are the same settings as in **Settings**, so a change in either place shows in both.

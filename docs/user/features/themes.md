# Color themes

Pick the colors JellyRock uses: one of its ready-made themes, or your own colors for the background, the highlights and the text. Each user on your Roku has a theme of their own.

## Pick a theme

1. Open **Settings**, then **User Interface**, then **General**, then **Theme**.
2. Pick a theme.
3. Leave Settings. Home rebuilds in the new colors.

The themes are **JellyRock**, **JellyRock (Black)**, **Emerald**, **Ember**, **Rose**, **Ocean**, **Dusk**, **Midnight**, **High Contrast** and **Custom**.

## Set your own colors

Pick **Custom**, then open **Custom Theme Colors** below **Theme**. Each color is a six-digit hex code, like `8B5CF6`:

- **Background - Primary**: the main background behind everything.
- **Background - Secondary**: cards, panels, buttons and popups.
- **Primary**: the focus border and highlights that show where you are.
- **Secondary**: selected tabs, progress bars and other accents.
- **Text - Primary**, **Text - Secondary** and **Text - Disabled**: the main text, smaller hints, and items you can't select.

A code that isn't six hex digits is ignored, and that color stays as it was.

## Settings

- [Theme](../app-settings.md#uiTheme) and [Custom Theme Colors](../app-settings.md#uiThemeColorBackgroundPrimary).
- [Backdrop Images](../app-settings.md#uiShowBackdrop): turn off the large background pictures for a plainer screen.

## Troubleshooting

- **The sign-in screens are in the standard colors.** That's expected: your theme applies once you sign in, and signing out goes back to the standard colors.

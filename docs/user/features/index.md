# Features

What JellyRock does, one page per feature. Each page says what the feature needs and how to use it.

| Feature | What you get | Jellyfin server | Server plugin |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Cast from another Jellyfin app](cast-to-jellyrock.md) | Cast from a Jellyfin app on your phone or browser to your Roku, then pause, seek and skip from there. | 10.7.0 or later (HTTPS: 10.9.0 or later) | None (HTTPS: JellyRock Companion) |
| [Color themes](themes.md) | Pick a color theme for JellyRock, or set every color yourself. | 10.7.0 or later | None |
| [Deep links for scripts and smart homes](deep-linking.md) | Open or play anything in JellyRock from another device, a script or a voice routine, with only the item's ID. | 10.7.0 or later | None |
| [Delete media](delete-media.md) | Delete a movie, an episode or any other item from your server, right from your Roku, if your account allows it. | 10.7.0 or later | None |
| [Favorites](favorites.md) | Mark what you love as a favorite and find it all in one place, on its own tab on Home. | 10.7.0 or later | None |
| [HDR and Dolby Vision](hdr-dolby-vision.md) | Watch HDR10, HDR10+, HLG and Dolby Vision as they were made, on a 4K Roku and a TV that shows them. | 10.7.0 or later | None |
| [Live TV and recordings](live-tv.md) | Watch live channels from your Jellyfin server, browse the TV guide, and schedule recordings from your couch. | 10.7.0 or later | None |
| [Manage subtitles](manage-subtitles.md) | Search your server's subtitle providers and download a subtitle onto a movie or episode from its page. | 10.7.0 or later | Open Subtitles |
| [Media segments (skip intro and outro)](media-segments.md) | Skip the intros, outros, recaps, previews and commercials your server has marked, or get a button to skip them. | 10.10.0 or later | Chapter Segments Provider or Intro Skipper |
| [Music](music.md) | Browse your music by artist, album or genre, play albums and instant mixes, and read a song's lyrics. | 10.7.0 or later | None |
| [Photos](photos.md) | Look through your photo albums on the TV, one by one or as a slideshow. | 10.7.0 or later | None |
| [Quick Connect (sign in with a code)](quick-connect.md) | Sign in with a short code from your phone or computer, instead of typing a password with the remote. | 10.7.0 or later | None |
| [Surround sound](surround-sound.md) | Hear 5.1 and 7.1 sound on your soundbar or receiver, kept as surround even when the server has to convert it. | 10.7.0 or later | None |
| [SyncPlay (watch together)](syncplay.md) | Watch together. Join a SyncPlay group and play, pause and seek in step with people on other Jellyfin apps. | 10.7.0 or later (HTTPS: 10.11.0 or later) | None (HTTPS: JellyRock Companion) |
| [Trickplay (preview thumbnails while seeking)](trickplay.md) | See preview pictures of the scene you're skipping to while you fast-forward or rewind. | 10.9.0 or later | None |
| [Voice control](voice-control.md) | Search by voice, and play, pause, seek, skip and replay with your Roku voice remote. | 10.7.0 or later | None |
| [Watch in original quality](original-quality.md) | Play your files as they are, with no conversion, whenever your Roku can, and see why when it can't. | 10.7.0 or later | None |

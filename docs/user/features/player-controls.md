# Player controls

While a video plays, one bar holds everything you need: where you are, what's next, and the audio, subtitles and chapters of what you're watching.

## Show and hide the controls

- Press **Up**, **Down** or **OK** to show the controls. The video keeps playing.
- Press **Back** to hide them. They also hide by themselves when you stop pressing buttons, unless the video is paused.
- Press **Play** at any time to pause or resume, with or without the controls on screen.

## What the controls show

- The title of what's playing, with its logo when your server has one.
- How far in you are, how much is left and when it **Ends at**. [Hide Clock](../app-settings.md#uiDesignHideClock) hides the end time.

## The buttons

Move along the row with **Left** and **Right**, and press **OK** to select. A button shows only when it applies to what's playing:

- **Previous and next:** go to the previous or next item in your queue. They're grayed out when there is none. With **Repeat all** on, they wrap around at both ends of the queue; with **Repeat one**, either plays the same video again and a message says so.
- **Play and pause.**
- **Version:** switch between the versions of a video that has more than one.
- **Audio:** switch to another audio track. A video never starts on a commentary track while it has another one, so choose it here to hear it. Audio description is skipped the same way, unless the file marks it as its default track (with [Play Default Audio Track](../app-settings.md#playbackPlayDefaultAudioTrack) on) or your Roku's Audio Guide is on.
- **Subtitles:** turn subtitles on or off, or pick another track.
- **Chapters:** open the video's chapter list.
- **LIVE:** on a live channel, go back to the live broadcast. See [Live TV and recordings](live-tv.md).

When the row doesn't fit on screen, the last buttons move into a **More** menu.

Two more buttons sit at the right of the bar. The gear lowers the quality for this video and sets **Repeat**, and the info button opens **Playback Info**. See [Watch in original quality](original-quality.md).

## Repeat

In the gear menu, pick **Repeat**, then **Off**, **Repeat all** or **Repeat one**. The menu row then reads **Repeat (All)** or **Repeat (One)**.

- **Repeat all** plays the queue again from its first item when the last one ends. A single video plays again.
- **Repeat one** plays the video that is on again when it ends, and the **Next Episode** prompt stays away.

During a Cinema Mode intro, **Repeat one** plays the video the intro is for, not the intro. Previous and next start that video too, with no message.

Your pick lasts for this video and the rest of what you queued with it; the next thing you play starts with repeat off. While repeat is on, or a quality cap is set, the gear shows in the highlight color. Music has its own repeat button on the [now-playing screen](music.md).

In a [SyncPlay](syncplay.md) group, **Repeat** sets the group's repeat mode, so it changes what plays next for everyone, and the gear gains **Shuffle**, **SyncPlay Time Offset** and **Leave Group**.

## Seek

With the controls hidden, press **Left**, **Right**, **Fast Forward** or **Rewind** to seek. When your server has the images, you see [preview pictures](trickplay.md) as you go.

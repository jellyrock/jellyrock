# Item details

Everything you select opens the same page: a movie, a show, a season, an episode, an artist, an album, a song, a person, a collection, a playlist, a photo album, a channel or a program. Once you know one, you know them all.

## What's on the page

- The title, its details, and the overview.
- A row of buttons for what you can do with this item.
- For a video, menus to pick its **Video** version, **Audio** track and **Subtitles** before you play it.
- Rows of related items below. Press **Down** from the buttons to reach them.

## The buttons

A button shows only when it applies to the item:

- **Play**, **Play All** or **Resume**, and **Shuffle** for what has more than one item.
- **Watched** and **Favorite**, which update your server as you press them. See [Favorites](favorites.md).
- **Instant Mix** on an artist, album or song. See [Music](music.md).
- **Play Trailer** when the item has one.
- **Go to Series**, **Go to Album**, **Go to Artist** or **Go to Channel**, to move up from an episode, a song, an album or a program.
- **Manage Subtitles**, **Delete** and **Record**, when your account may use them. See [Manage subtitles](manage-subtitles.md), [Delete media](delete-media.md) and [Live TV and recordings](live-tv.md).

When the row doesn't fit on screen, the last buttons move into a **More** menu.

## The rows below

The rows depend on the item, and a row with nothing to show stays hidden. A movie can have **Chapters**, **Additional Parts**, **Cast & Crew**, **Special Features** and **More Like This**. A show has its **Seasons**, a person their movies and episodes, and an artist their albums and songs.

On Jellyfin 12.0 or later, **Collections** lists the collections the item is in.

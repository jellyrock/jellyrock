# Favorites

Mark movies, shows, music, channels, people and more as favorites, and find them all together on the **Favorites** tab on Home. Favorites are saved on your Jellyfin server, so they're the same in every Jellyfin app you use.

## Mark a favorite

- On an item's page, press **Favorite**. Press it again to take the item off your favorites.
- While something plays, ask your Roku voice remote to **like** it to add it, or **dislike** it to take it off. See [Voice control](voice-control.md).

## Find your favorites

- **On Home**, select the **Favorites** tab at the top of the screen. Your favorites are grouped in rows by type: movies, shows, episodes, people, artists, albums, songs, channels, photos and the rest.
- **In a library**, press the options button (**\***) and filter it to your favorites.
- **In the Live TV guide**, the same filter shows only your favorite channels. See [Live TV and recordings](live-tv.md).

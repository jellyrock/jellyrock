# Languages

JellyRock has its own translations, so its language doesn't depend on the languages your Roku offers. Each user on your Roku can have their own.

## Choose your language

1. Open **Settings**, then **User Interface**, then **General**, then **Display Language**.
2. Search for your language and pick it. The change takes effect when you leave Settings.

Leave it on **Automatic** to follow your Jellyfin language preference. With none set, JellyRock uses your Roku's language.

## The sign-in screens

Nobody is signed in yet on the screens before Home, so they have a setting of their own: [Sign-in Screen Language](../app-settings.md#globalTranslationLocale). **Automatic** uses your Roku's language.

## Text that isn't translated yet

Text with no translation in your language shows in English.

## Help translate

Translations live on [JellyRock's Weblate](https://translate.jellyrock.app/projects/jellyrock). Add a missing string there, or fix one that reads wrong.

## Settings

- [Display Language](../app-settings.md#translationLocale): the language of the app's text, for each user.
- [Sign-in Screen Language](../app-settings.md#globalTranslationLocale): the language before anyone signs in.
- [Language Preferences](../app-settings.md#playback-language-preferences): which audio and subtitle tracks to prefer. These choose tracks in your media, not the app's text.

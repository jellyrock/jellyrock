# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the
JellyRock community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation. We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open,
welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standard

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1][covenant], and that
document is the full, governing text. In short:

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive. Assume good faith.
- Welcome newcomers and differing viewpoints; give and accept feedback
  graciously.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.
- Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, personal or political attacks,
  publishing others' private information without permission, and other behavior
  a reasonable person would consider inappropriate. Maintainers may remove,
  edit, or reject contributions that don't align with this Code, and may
  temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behavior they deem
  inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code applies in all community spaces — the repository, issues, pull
requests, and the project's chat — and when an individual is officially
representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the project
maintainer, [@cewert](https://github.com/cewert). All reports will be reviewed
and investigated promptly and fairly, and the maintainer will respect the
privacy and security of the reporter.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines][ladder]
(correction → warning → temporary ban → permanent ban), applied at the
maintainer's discretion in proportion to the violation.
